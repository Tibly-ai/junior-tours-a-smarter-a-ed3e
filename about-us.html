Our Story

Founded in 1976 by Marty Abrams, a former Brooklyn school teacher, Junior Tours - A Smarter Approach to Student Travel began with a simple belief: travel opens a young person's world in a way no textbook can. Marty started the company with a classroom full of students, a vision, and a determination to give them experiences they'd carry for the rest of their lives.

From that first trip, the goal never changed. We wanted teachers to trust us with their students, and students to come home talking about what they saw and learned.
