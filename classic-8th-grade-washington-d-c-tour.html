Washington D.C. 8th Grade Tradition

A Signature Experience for Middle School Students in Our Nation's Capital

For 8th grade students across the country, a trip to Washington, D.C. is the capstone experience that marks the end of middle school. Every spring, more than a million students make this trip. The days on the bus, the inside jokes at the memorials, and the late-night hotel hallway laughter stay with them for years.

Sample Itinerary: Day 1

Departure from your school (or local airport if the group is flying)

  • 1:00 PM Arrive in Washington D.C., meet your tour escort, visit Arlington National Cemetery, watch the Changing of the Guard at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, and visit the gravesites of the Kennedy brothers and the Challenger crew.
  • 3:30 PM Visit the Iwo Jima Marine Corps Memorial, the Air Force Memorial, and the 9/11 Pentagon Memorial.

Day 2

  • 8:00 AM Breakfast at the hotel
  • 10:00 AM Tour of the U.S. Capitol Building, plus the Supreme Court and Library of Congress
  • 12:30 PM Lunch at the group's expense, followed by Smithsonian museum visits on the National Mall: African American History and Culture, Air & Space, American History, Natural History, and/or the National Gallery of Art
  • 4:30 PM Visit the Daniel Chester French sculpture and evening free time

Day 3

  • 8:00 AM Breakfast at the hotel and check out
  • 10:00 AM Visit the National Archives to see the Declaration of Independence, the Constitution, and the Bill of Rights
  • 11:30 AM Visit Ford's Theatre and the Petersen House
  • 1:00 PM Lunch at the group's expense
  • 2:00 PM Visit The People's House: A White House Experience
  • 3:00 PM Ride down Embassy Row, past the Washington National Cathedral
  • 4:00 PM Approximate departure time for home

Optional Extension: 4-Day Package

Evening of Day 3

  • 5:30 PM Dinner
  • 7:00 PM Rooftop views from the Kennedy Center, then a visit to Georgetown
  • 9:30 PM Return to the hotel with hallway security monitoring overnight

Day 4

  • 8:00 AM Breakfast at the hotel and check out
  • 10:00 AM Admission to Mount Vernon, the home and birthplace of George Washington
  • Lunch at the group's expense, followed by afternoon departure for home
