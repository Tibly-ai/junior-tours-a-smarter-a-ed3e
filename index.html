Who We Are

Three decades of getting the details right, so teachers don't have to.

Junior Tours - A Smarter Approach to Student Travel started as a two-person operation booking bus trips for a handful of Midwest schools. Today we still answer our own phones, but we've planned thousands of trips to Washington D.C., New York City, Orlando, and beyond, for drama clubs, honor societies, and entire 8th grade classes.

Every itinerary is built around your curriculum and your budget, not a template. We vet every hotel and motor coach ourselves, keep a planner assigned to your trip from the first call, and stay reachable while your group is on the road.

Less paperwork for you. More learning for them.
