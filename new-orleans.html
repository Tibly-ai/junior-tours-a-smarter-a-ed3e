New Orleans

New Orleans is a mood as much as it is a destination. The city's French and Spanish roots, its Cajun and Creole cooking, and a musical legacy that runs deep all mix together into something you won't find anywhere else. Whether your group is walking through the French Quarter or gliding along the bayou on a riverboat, students come home with stories they'll tell for years. NOLA is known for its jazz, its history, and a warmth that's hard to describe until you feel it firsthand.
