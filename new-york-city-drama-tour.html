The premier travel option for drama and theatre teachers

With its vibrant Theatre District, world-class Broadway productions, and endless educational resources, it's no wonder hundreds of thousands of students visit New York City every year. A theatre trip to NYC leaves a mark that never fades. Your students will step into the world of professional performers, see the craft up close, and come home with stories they'll tell for years.

Signature Workshops: Built to Inspire Young Artists

Improvisation for the Unsuspecting

A beginner improv session that builds playfulness, trust, and self-awareness. Students take risks, make surprising choices, and practice group and solo scenes.

Improvisation, Next Session

A fast-paced improv workshop that strengthens trust, confidence, and risk-taking. Students build on skills from the introductory session.

Showstopper: A Working Musical Theater Party

Students learn, stage, and perform a new musical number under tight rehearsal deadlines, just like Broadway. A fun crash course in working quickly and professionally.

Stage Combat 101

Students learn safe slaps, falls, and basic fight techniques from professional stunt coordinators. Hands-on training with safety mats prepares them for combat scenes on stage or screen.

Dancing With The Stars

Students learn choreography with a professional dancer specializing in Salsa, Jazz, Hip Hop, Contemporary, and more. Big style, big energy, plenty of personality.

Broadway Musicals and the Joy of Singing

Participants work with a Broadway vocal coach to build confidence, power, and joy in singing. Volunteers may be asked to perform a short solo for the group.

FAQs About Drama Student Trips to New York City

How far in advance should I plan a NYC drama trip?

Most drama teachers book 8 to 12 months ahead for the best Broadway tickets, workshop slots, and hotel rates. Early planning also gives you time for fundraising and coordinating family schedules.

What's the average group size for student travel to New York City?

Group sizes vary widely, from a single classroom to a full grade level. We adjust logistics to fit your group.

Are workshops customizable for specific drama skills?

Yes. We work with your teacher to select topics that match your curriculum and student interests.

Optimal Timing for Student Trips to New York City

Timing your drama student travel to New York City affects availability, cost, and weather.

  • January to February: budget-friendly, fewer crowds, good for the most affordable trips
  • March to June: ideal for school groups, new shows opening, abundant workshop availability
  • October to November: festive atmosphere, great for holiday-themed drama trips
