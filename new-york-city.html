New York City

New York City stands alone among the world's destinations for its density, its diversity, and the sheer pace of its cultural and economic life. Students feel the energy the moment they step off the bus. Junior Tours - A Smarter Approach to Student Travel has been guiding student groups through NYC for years, with a home base near the heart of the action so your group loses no time getting where it needs to go.

New York City Landmarks

Statue of Liberty

This iconic symbol of freedom was a gift from the people of France in 1886. Made of copper, Lady Liberty holds a book inscribed July 4, 1776 in one hand and a torch of freedom in the other.

Central Park

Central Park is the first public park built in America. Across its 850-plus acres, popular stops include Strawberry Fields and Bethesda Terrace.

Times Square

The crossroads of the world, lit up day and night with billboards, theaters, and street performers. It is one of the most photographed spots on any student itinerary.

Empire State Building

Standing 102 stories tall, the Empire State Building held the title of world's tallest office building for over 40 years. The 1931 Art Deco tower offers views of up to 80 miles on a clear day.

St. Patrick's Cathedral

Completed in 1879, this Neo-Gothic cathedral has long served as a cornerstone of Catholic life in the United States.

Rockefeller Center

A complex of Art Deco buildings and public plazas at the heart of Midtown, home to the famous ice rink and observation deck.

United Nations

Headquartered on an 18-acre site on Manhattan's East Side, visitors here explore historic chambers, learn how global decisions are made, and view exhibits on the UN's peacekeeping and humanitarian work.

Federal Hall

At a time when New York City was the nation's capital, George Washington took the oath of office here as the first President of the United States.

Wall Street

The financial heart of the country, where the Charging Bull statue and the New York Stock Exchange draw crowds every day.

Grand Central Terminal

Built of glass and steel, this 100-foot wide by 650-foot long structure rivaled the Eiffel Tower and Crystal Palace as a feat of 19th-century engineering.

Intrepid Sea, Air & Space Museum

Visitors explore the historic aircraft carrier USS Intrepid, view iconic aircraft, and see the Space Shuttle Enterprise up close.

One World Observatory

Rising above the World Trade Center site, the observatory offers sweeping 360-degree views of the entire city.

Drama Programs

Broadway

The Great White Way is home to 41 theaters showcasing the very best of live theatrical performance.

Workshops

Master classes in acting, improvisation, musical theatre, and vocal training are offered by Broadway professionals in a private studio setting, giving your students real, hands-on training.

Radio City Music Hall

Located in Rockefeller Center, this Art Deco showplace hosts performances and behind-the-scenes tours students won't forget.
