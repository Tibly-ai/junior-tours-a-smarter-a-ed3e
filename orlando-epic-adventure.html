STARS Performance Programs & Educational Tours

Beyond the fun, Universal Studios has invested heavily in youth and educational programs that turn a theme park visit into a real learning experience. Performing arts groups can take the stage live in front of hundreds of guests, or work through hands-on workshops with industry professionals covering improvisation, vocal technique, and behind-the-scenes production.

Dance Workshop

Dance teams and musical theatre programs work directly with a Universal choreographer to learn original choreography inspired by Universal's movies, TV shows, and parades. Students gain insight into performance quality, stage presence, rehearsal technique, and what it takes to bring a high-energy routine to life on a professional stage.

Choir / Vocal Workshop

Bring a piece your group has been rehearsing and work with a Universal vocal coach on technique, blend, and stage presence, then take what you've learned into a live performance opportunity in the park.

FAQs About Student Trips to Universal Orlando

How far in advance should we plan this trip?

We recommend planning 8 to 12 months ahead so there's time to get administrator approval, advertise the trip, spread out payments, and lock in hotels and educational opportunities.

What's the ideal group size, and do trip leaders have to handle money?

Junior Tours - A Smarter Approach to Student Travel handles collections, so trip leaders don't have to chase down payments or hold cash. Groups of any size are welcome, and we'll help you scale the itinerary to fit.

Best Time of Year for Student Trips to Orlando

  • September to February: Great for budget-minded groups that want fewer crowds and an escape from cold winters back home.
  • March to June: Popular for spring break trips or end-of-year celebrations.
